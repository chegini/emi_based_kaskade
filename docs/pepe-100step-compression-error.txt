Pepe 100-step compression-error experiment
========================================
Submit tests/submit_emi_pepe_100steps.sbatch from the EMI repository root.
8 Gold6342 nodes, 16 ranks, 64 GB/node, six-hour job limit.
Builds a separate executable; each trajectory has a 150-minute limit.

Comparison: raw FCG (split off) versus 16-bit FCG with constant splitting,
transform before quantization, destination routing, local bypass off.
Both evolve their OWN state over 100 steps from identical initial conditions.
dt=0.01, finalTime=1.0 in model time units. All steps must meet true residual
1e-7. No direct solve and no assumed scientific solution-error threshold.
The explicit FCG split experiment now accepts 1..100 steps; ordinary CG
remains limited to one step and the FGMRES limit is unchanged.

Outputs under output/emi-pepe-100steps-JOB/trajectory:
 compression-error.csv: per-step global coefficient Euclidean error,
 maximum absolute difference, mean drift, supplementary centered error,
 iterations and true residuals. Raw error is not replaced by centered error.
 difference-step100.csv: full-precision final DOF differences.
 summary.json: completion state and final error.
 fcg-uncompressed/emiStep100.vtu and fcg-before-16-split-on/emiStep100.vtu:
 final VTK fields for visual inspection (also emiLast and earlier steps).

VTK export uses seven-digit precision and nonconforming points. Numerical
error is therefore computed from full-precision, globally indexed CSV
snapshots, not rounded VTK point values. The norm is a coefficient norm,
not an FE mass-weighted L2 norm or a membrane-voltage error norm. This is a
compression sensitivity experiment, not a time-discretization accuracy
study or performance benchmark. Check the entire curve, not just step 100.

Local validation: C++ option boundary tests, generated Slurm/application
arguments, and synthetic error calculations. Full MPI build/run is performed
by the submitted job. A small error is an outcome to assess, not a pass rule.
